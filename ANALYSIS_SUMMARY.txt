================================================================================
KEY INSIGHTS FOR ALGORITHM DESIGN
================================================================================

1. STATISTICAL CHALLENGES:
   - Small corpus (145 docs) limits statistical power
   - Sparse data (many terms <5 occurrences)
   - Need robust methods for low-frequency events
   
2. SHIFT PATTERNS:
   - Most shifts are removals (108 vs 22 emergences)
   - Terms often cycle on/off (not permanent changes)
   - Major shifts cluster around policy regime changes
   
3. CONTEXT MATTERS:
   - Same word has different meanings over time
   - "Transitory" in 2015 ≠ "transitory" in 2021
   - Need to distinguish intermittent use from sustained signals
   
4. MULTI-WORD PHRASES:
   - Key signals often multi-word ("substantial further progress")
   - Single-word tracking may miss important shifts
   - Need n-gram or phrase detection capability

5. TEMPORAL AUTOCORRELATION:
   - Policy language is highly autocorrelated
   - Statements intentionally repeat previous language
   - True shifts are deviations from this repetition
